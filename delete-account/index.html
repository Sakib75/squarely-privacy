Delete your Squarely account

Squarely is a shared-expense tracker published by Nazmus Sakib. This page explains how to delete your account and exactly what is removed.

Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open Squarely and sign in.
  2. Tap the Account tab (bottom right).
  3. Scroll to Danger zone.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Read the summary, type DELETE to confirm, and tap Delete my account.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out. You will not be able to sign in again.

Settle up first. If you still owe money to someone, or someone owes you, Squarely will refuse the deletion and show you the outstanding amounts. This protects the other people you share expenses with, whose balances are calculated from your shared records. Record the payments to bring every balance to zero, then delete.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email sakibkuet16@gmail.com from the address registered to your account, with the subject “Squarely account deletion request”. We will verify ownership and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Name, email address, profile photoDeleted
Sign-in credentials (password)Deleted — you can no longer log in
Friend connections and group membershipsDeleted
Notifications and registered devices (push tokens)Deleted
Recurring expense templates you createdDeleted
Expenses and settlements you shared with other peopleKept, with your name replaced by “Deleted user”

Why some records are kept. A shared expense belongs to everyone on it. Other members' balances are calculated from those rows, so deleting them would silently change what your friends believe they owe each other. Those retained records carry no personal identifiers after deletion — your name, email and photo are removed, and the entry shows only “Deleted user”.

Retention period

Personal data is removed immediately when the account is deleted. Encrypted database backups may still contain it for up to 30 days before they rotate out, after which no copy remains. The anonymised shared expense records described above are kept indefinitely, because other users' balances depend on them.
